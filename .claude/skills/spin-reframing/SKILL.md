# Spin reframing

Satire inspired by a retired CEO in *This American Life* #436 who recast checklist traits as leadership virtues.

## Steps
1. Take the character's three highest trait themes.
2. For each, write a one-line LinkedIn-style reframe, e.g. Short Fuse -> "Bias for decisive action." Con Artistry -> "World-class stakeholder management."
3. End with a mock endorsement or job title ("Chief Disruption Officer").
4. Add a one-line wink so nobody takes it seriously: "(Spin Doctor's opinions are satire. The Judge is not impressed.)"

## Rules
- Fictional characters only. Never spin real people or real CEOs.
- Punch at the corporate jargon, not at victims in the story.
