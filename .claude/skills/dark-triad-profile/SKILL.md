# Dark Triad profile

Based on Paulhus & Williams (2002): three overlapping but distinct, non-clinical "dark" traits sharing a core of callous manipulation.

| Trait | Shorthand | Theme signals |
|---|---|---|
| Machiavellianism | The schemer | high Con Artistry, long-term planning, cool control (lower Short Fuse) |
| Narcissism | The egoist | high Main Character Energy and Charm Offensive, craves admiration |
| Psychopathy | The cold predator | high Cold Heart, Remorse Meter, Thrill Hunger, Short Fuse |

## Steps
1. From the theme scores, rate each trait **low / moderate / high**.
2. Name the **dominant flavor** in one line: "A schemer, not a predator."
3. Mention one thing that pushes *against* the profile (e.g., real love for someone, visible guilt).

## Rules
- Present these as flavors, not diagnoses; the three overlap heavily in research.
