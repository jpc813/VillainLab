# Quiz design

## Requirements
- **12 original questions**, everyday and lighthearted (e.g., "A friend cries at a movie. You: ..."). Never adapted from PCL-R items or interview guides.
- Each question maps to one of the eight trait themes; cover every theme at least once.
- 3-4 answer options, scored 0-3. Mix which option is "edgy" so it isn't obvious.
- Scoring happens in plain JavaScript in the browser. No network calls, no storage.

## Result tiers (examples, keep them kind)
- Low: "Too neurotic to be a psychopath. Congratulations, you have a conscience."
- Middle: "Charming rogue. Mildly mischievous, deeply fine."
- High: "Suspiciously chill. Probably just very relaxed, but maybe call your mom."
Every tier ends with: "For entertainment only, not a diagnosis. Your answers never left this page."

## Themes
| Theme | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| Charm Offensive | Smooth, flattering, wins people over fast |
| Main Character Energy | Sees self as superior, special, entitled |
| Con Artistry | Lies, schemes, uses people as tools |
| Remorse Meter | Shows guilt or regret? Scored inverted: 10 = no remorse at all |
| Cold Heart | Little warmth or empathy for others' pain |
| Thrill Hunger | Bored easily, chases risk and excitement |
| Short Fuse | Impulsive, explosive, poor self-control |
| Track Record | Pattern of harmful acts across the story |
