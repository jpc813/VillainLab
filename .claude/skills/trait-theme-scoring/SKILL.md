# Trait theme scoring

The Villain Lab's eight trait themes are **our own original groupings**. They are NOT PCL-R items, wording or criteria, and must never be described as such.

| Theme | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| Charm Offensive | Smooth, flattering, wins people over fast |
| Main Character Energy | Sees self as superior, special, entitled |
| Con Artistry | Lies, schemes, uses people as tools |
| Remorse Meter | Shows guilt or regret? Scored inverted: 10 = no remorse at all |
| Cold Heart | Little warmth or empathy for others' pain |
| Thrill Hunger | Bored easily, chases risk and excitement |
| Short Fuse | Impulsive, explosive, poor self-control |
| Track Record | Pattern of harmful acts across the story |

## How to score
1. Read the character's evidence file (from `canon-evidence`). Score only what happens in the story.
2. Give each theme an integer **0-10**:
   - 0-2: little or no sign of it
   - 3-5: present sometimes or mildly
   - 6-8: a clear, repeated pattern
   - 9-10: defines the character
3. For every theme, cite **one or two specific moments** as evidence, plus one counter-example if one exists.
4. **Villain Index** = (sum of the eight scores / 80) x 100, rounded to a whole number.
5. Index bands (for display only): 0-24 "Mostly harmless", 25-49 "Morally murky", 50-74 "Genuinely dangerous", 75-100 "Full villain".

## Rules
- Never write "is a psychopath" or "diagnosis." Say "scores high on the Cold Heart theme" or "shows a callous pattern."
- If you are a rater with an assigned side, you may *emphasize* evidence for your side, but you may **not invent** events.
- Always attach the label: "For entertainment only, not a diagnosis."
