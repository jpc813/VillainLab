# Copyright check

## Block
- Any PCL-R item descriptions, scoring criteria, interview questions or manual text (MHS copyright, Qualification Level C).
- Quotes over 15 words, or more than one quote per source.
- Copied passages from the episode transcript, Ronson's book or any novel or script.
- Song lyrics of any length.

## Allowed
- General published facts: the PCL-R has 20 items scored 0-2, totals 0-40, ~30 cut-off, two broad factors.
- Our own eight trait themes and original quiz questions.
- Character names and our own descriptions of plot events.

## How to check
Search the diff for long quoted strings, the phrase "PCL-R item," and any text that reads like a manual. If in doubt, paraphrase or cut.
