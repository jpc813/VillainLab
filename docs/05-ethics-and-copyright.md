# Ethics and copyright rules

These are project requirements, enforced by the **Ethics & Safety Reviewer** agent.

## 1. Never reproduce the real PCL-R

- The **Hare PCL-R: 2nd Edition** is a commercial, copyrighted instrument published by **MHS** (also sold through Pearson). Its manual, rating booklet, interview guides and forms are sold only to qualified buyers.
- It is a **Qualification Level C** product: buyers need an advanced degree in an appropriate field (e.g., psychology, psychiatry) plus training and experience with tests, and must sign an attestation before purchase.
- MHS requires permission to reproduce its copyrighted material (permissions@mhs.com).

**So:** the app, the agents' knowledge folders and the quiz never contain PCL-R item descriptions, scoring criteria, interview questions or anything copied from the manual. General, widely published facts are fine (20 items, 0/1/2 scale, 0–40 range, ~30 cut-off, two broad factors). The quiz and the raters use **our own original trait themes and questions**.

## 2. A web quiz cannot diagnose anyone

A real PCL-R assessment takes a trained clinician, a 90–120 minute interview and an hour of record review. The episode's own staff test, done casually, came back all zeros. The app must therefore:

- label every result **"For entertainment only, not a diagnosis"**;
- never use words like "diagnosis," "you are a psychopath," or "clinical";
- point anyone worried about their mental health to a professional, gently, on the About page.

## 3. No rating real people

The episode shows how a label can follow a real person for decades (the parole story) and how tempting it is to see psychopaths everywhere (the CEO story). So:

- The **Villain Panel only accepts fictional characters.** If a user enters a real person (a celebrity, politician, coworker, ex, family member), the agents politely decline and suggest a fictional character instead.
- There is **no "rate someone you know" mode**, anywhere.
- The self-quiz is something a person takes **only about themselves**.
- In the docs, private individuals and radio staff from the episode use **fictitious names**.

## 4. Quoting sources

- Summarize episodes, papers and books **in our own words**. Short quotes only when essential, always attributed.
- Link everything from the app's **Resources** page.
