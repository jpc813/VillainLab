# Class demo guide (remote, over Zoom)

## Before class

- Deploy and test the live site. Confirm the 18 pre-generated villain panels load even with the API turned off.
- Pick 2–3 villains for the live panel. Good choices: one clear-cut (Hannibal Lecter), one ambiguous (Cersei Lannister), one redemption arc (Zuko or Scrooge).
- Optional: create an **anonymous Zoom poll** with playful buckets, e.g., "Neurotic angel / Mildly mischievous / Charming rogue / Please don't ask."

## Running the demo (about 15 minutes)

1. **Share your screen on the Villain Panel only.** Let the class pick a villain from the list.
2. Run it. Point out the **gap** between the Prosecution and Defense scores and read the Judge's explanation: same evidence, different conclusions. This is the episode's big finding live.
3. Try the **Spin Doctor** on the same villain for a laugh.
4. Try **Then vs. Now** on a redemption-arc character.
5. **Stop sharing.** Drop the link in chat and invite everyone to take the self-quiz **privately on their own computer**.
6. Run the anonymous poll if you made one, and show only the group totals.

## Tips to read out to classmates

- Please **don't share your screen** while you take the quiz.
- Please **don't post your score** in the chat or say it out loud. Class sessions are often recorded and chats are saved.
- The quiz runs only in your browser; your answers aren't sent or saved. Refresh to clear it, or use **Hide my result**.
- It's for fun, not a diagnosis. Like the radio staff in the episode, most of us are "too neurotic to be psychopaths."

## For the instructor

The quiz link can be shared with anyone; each person takes it on themselves. Showing your own result to the class is entirely your call.
