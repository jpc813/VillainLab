# Starter villains and antiheroes

The Villain Panel works with any fictional character, but these 18 are pre-loaded (and can be pre-generated so the demo works without live API calls). They are chosen to give the raters something to argue about: some clear-cut, some ambiguous, some with redemption arcs for the **Then vs. Now** analyst.

Notes are short, our own words, and describe behavior within the story only.

| # | Character | Source | Why they're interesting | Arc? |

## Rules for characters

- **Fictional only.** Characters based closely on real people (biopics, true crime) are refused, the same as real people.
- When characters differ across versions (book vs. film, comic runs), the Literary Analyst states which version the panel is judging.
- Spoilers: the panel warns before discussing endings or twists.
