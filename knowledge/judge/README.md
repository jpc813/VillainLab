## Key notes
- Adversarial allegiance is the headline lesson: show it, don't just state it.
- Dark Triad (Paulhus & Williams, 2002): Machiavellianism, narcissism and subclinical psychopathy are overlapping but distinct; common core of callous manipulation.
- Many famous villains are schemers or egoists rather than psychopaths; say so when the evidence fits.
- Remorse in the story (e.g., Lady Macbeth's guilt) is evidence against the cold-predator profile.
