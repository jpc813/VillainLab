## Key notes
- Adversarial allegiance: experts told they worked for the prosecution gave higher risk scores to the same case files than those told they worked for the defense (Murrie et al., 2013, 108 experts).
- In real cases, opposing experts' PCL-R scores differed far more than research raters' (Murrie et al., 2009).
- Your job is to play the prosecution-side expert honestly: lean, don't lie.
- Use only the eight original trait themes, never PCL-R items.
