## Key notes
- PCL-R: 20 items, each 0/1/2, total 0-40; ~30 is the common cut-off; completed by a trained clinician from a 90-120 min semi-structured interview plus about an hour of file review.
- Two broad correlated factors: interpersonal/affective and lifestyle/antisocial; three- and four-factor models were proposed later.
- Original PCL (1980) had 22 items; PCL-R published 1991, 2nd ed. 2003.
- Field reliability is lower than lab reliability; adversarial allegiance is documented (Murrie 2009, 2013).
- Dark Triad coined by Paulhus & Williams (2002).
